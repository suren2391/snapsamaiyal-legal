Privacy Policy

Welcome to SnapSamaiyal ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SnapSamaiyal mobile application.

By using SnapSamaiyal, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Email address — when you sign up or sign in using email OTP.
  • Phone number — if you choose to sign in using phone OTP.
  • Photos — images of your fridge or ingredients that you choose to upload for recipe generation. These are stored securely in our cloud storage.
  • Saved recipes — recipes you choose to save within the app.
  • Usage data — basic app usage information to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the SnapSamaiyal service.
  • Generate AI-powered recipe suggestions based on your ingredients.
  • Save and sync your recipes across sessions.
  • Send authentication OTP codes via email or SMS.
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Analyse app usage to improve features.

3. AI & Third-Party Services

SnapSamaiyal uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API — to analyse ingredient photos and generate recipes. Images are processed securely and not stored by Anthropic beyond the API call.
  • Supabase — for user authentication and database storage. Data is stored securely with row-level security.
  • Google AdMob — to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalisation. You can opt out via your device settings.
  • Expo / React Native — the app framework used to build the application.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure. We implement row-level security (RLS) so that each user can only access their own data. Fridge photos are stored in a private storage bucket accessible only to the uploading user.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data including saved recipes and uploaded images will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising via your device settings.

To delete your account, go to the Profile tab in the app and tap "Delete Account".

7. Children's Privacy

SnapSamaiyal is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads within the app. AdMob uses device identifiers to serve personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings. For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
